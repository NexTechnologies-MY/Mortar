# Industry Practitioner Survey Findings

Findings from the Google Form "Property Booking-to-SPA Conversion — Industry
Practitioner Survey", submitted anonymously by eight consenting practitioners
between 18 and 22 September 2026 (n = 8). This provides practitioner evidence to
rank booking leakage causes
([Problem Statement](/docs/source/problem-statement.md)) and informs the
[Front-End Simulation](/docs/research/company-brain/simulation.md) assumptions
panel. Findings reflect industry experience, not Chin Hin records.

**Earlier Counts:** R1 to R5 answered on 18 September and R6 to R8 on 20 and 22
September. Documents written before this update may still cite the first five
responses as n = 5.

## Implications For Mortar

**Ranked Leakage Causes:** Loan rejection is named in every top three (8 of 8)
and as the single biggest cause by 7 of 8, and bank credit assessment is where
bookings stall (5 of 8). This is our practitioner evidence for the proposal's
ranked causes, demonstrating how we know rather than guess.

**The Dissenting Expert:** R7, the only respondent covering loan administration
and legal, names slow bank processing as the biggest cause and buyer follow-up
as the stuck stage. Both are problems a chase workflow addresses directly, so
the banker-chasing side of Mortar is not a side feature.

**Appropriate AI Boundary:** The AI boundary matches the concept: AI suggests
next steps (4 of 8), checks documents (3 of 8) and summarises WhatsApp or banker
updates (3 of 8, all from R6 to R8), while people decide. The summarising answer
supports message extraction directly. Only one respondent would use AI to
estimate conversion risk, so the forecast stays statistical.

**The Financing Eligibility Gap:** Seven of eight want an early financing
eligibility check, and all eight rank financing as the top signal. Only three
say eligibility is always checked, which suggests today's checks are uneven,
late or shallow; the survey cannot say which. To address the Sales-momentum
tension, Mortar should compute an advisory financing-risk flag per booking from
the simulation's debt service ratio, margin-of-financing and tenure seed
values—a prompt to follow up, not a gate on bookings.

**Simulation Seed Calibration:** Conversion and timing answers are too scattered
to use as seed values. Half say 0–2 of every 10 bookings sign, while the rest
spread from 3–4 to 7–8 or are unsure. Booking to SPA is 3–4 weeks for four
respondents and 7 weeks or more for three. At most, these define a sensitivity
range on the assumptions panel, tagged "survey, n = 8".

**Tracking And Core Systems:** Tracking is spread across CRMs (4 of 8),
spreadsheets (2 of 8), WhatsApp (2 of 8), email (1 of 8) and property management
software (1 of 8). This supports one shared case record and spreadsheet import.
IFCA integration is a roadmap item, starting from read-only exports as the
concept recommends.

## Limitations

Eight self-selected respondents, mixed roles, self-reported general experience
rather than company records, and multi-select answers counted per respondent.
Only one respondent covers loan administration or legal work.
