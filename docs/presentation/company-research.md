# Chin Hin Company Research And Presentation Implications

Verified against primary company publications on 28 September 2026. This
briefing supports page 3 and the adoption discussion. It does not establish
internal booking performance. Public figures below are dated context, not claims
about the latest available quarter.

## Correct Company Identity

**Chin Hin Group Berhad** is the wider listed group spanning property,
construction engineering, building materials and home/living. Its official site
describes an integrated business ecosystem. **Chin Hin Group Property Berhad
(CHGP)** is the listed property business relevant to the challenge. Do not
combine their consolidated revenue, profit or pipeline figures.
[Group overview](https://www.chinhingroup.com/),
[property division](https://www.chinhingroup.com/property-development/),
[CHGP website](https://chinhinproperty.com/).

NexTechnologies-MY is the GitHub organisation containing Mortar. It is not the
challenge sponsor. Its public GitHub profile identifies Malaysia and links to
nextalentai.com; that alone does not establish a legal company identity or
trading history. Mortar is the solution being presented; Chin Hin is the
prospective pilot organisation.

## Business Model And Scale

CHGP's FY2025 annual report gives revenue of RM976.748 million, year-end
unbilled sales of RM2.18 billion, and a first-time-owner focus. It describes the
November 2024 disposal of its construction division and a sharper property
focus. Sources: printed pp. 2–3 and 10; PDF pages 3 and 7.
[Annual Report 2025](https://cms.chinhinproperty.com/uploads/WEBSITE_Chin_Hin_Group_Property_Berhad_AR_2025_8757f6df1c.pdf).

For a separately dated check, CHGP's unaudited first-quarter report gives
RM241.668 million consolidated revenue for the period ended 31 March 2026. The
property segment contributes RM233.077 million. The performance commentary
attributes recognition to project progress. These are different reporting scopes
from the wider group's quarter. Source: printed pp. 1, 9 and 12.
[CHGP 1Q2026 report](https://cms.chinhinproperty.com/uploads/CHGP_1st_Quarter_Report_31_Mar_2026_5299f60d95.pdf).

**Inference for Mortar:** validate unsigned booking conversion upstream of
contracted sales. Do not apply a hypothetical conversion uplift to unbilled
sales, multiply the full pipeline by a survey percentage, or treat unit value as
cash recovered. Cash requires billing and receipt evidence.

## What Management Publicly Prioritises

The wider group's 1Q2026 release stresses execution, cash conversion and
margins. It reports property unbilled sales of RM2.20 billion at 31 March 2026,
alongside a construction order book and home/living backlogs. Its RM5.41 billion
combined pipeline includes all those categories. That combined number is not
property bookings. The page header is dated 9 May while the release body is
dated 21 May; use the reporting cutoff, 31 March, when citing the figures.
[Group 1Q2026 release](https://www.chinhingroup.com/news/chin-hin-group-builds-strong-momentum-with-rm-68-1-million-1-qfy-26-pbt-and-rm-5-41-billion-order-pipeline/).

**Inference:** an executive is likely to ask about measurable execution,
ownership and integration. Show verified actions and cohort outcomes before
technology details. Do not claim the company needs another dashboard.

## Customer And Project Fit

The official CHGP website lists a varied portfolio including Ayanna, Avantro,
Aricia, Crown Penang, Dawn KLCC and Botanica Hills, with nature-themed,
trendy/fashionable and premium/sophisticated positioning. Do not assume one
buyer segment or financing threshold fits the whole portfolio.
[Official portfolio](https://chinhinproperty.com/).

The company's buyer guide explicitly includes comparing banks, preparing
documents and checking eligibility before signing agreements. It also offers a
mortgage calculator and warns that bank rates vary.
[First-homebuyer guide](https://chinhinproperty.com/first-homebuyer-guide/).

**Inference:** demonstrate a practical document/loan/legal handoff. Aster
Heights, Apex Bank and Raymond Tan are demo fixtures, not company evidence.
Choose a real pilot project with staff; do not assign one publicly named project
a leakage problem without internal evidence.

## Existing Transformation And Adoption

Chin Hin's 29 July 2026 announcement describes a partnership with Kingdee and a
common ERP backbone covering finance, procurement and supply chain, connected to
operational modules. It also stresses employee learning and AI assistance. The
announcement does not disclose the candidate project's booking interface,
available APIs or completion of rollout.
[One Chin Hin transformation](https://www.chinhingroup.com/news/shaping-the-next-chapter-the-one-chin-hin-transformation/).

**Inference:** ask about the existing master record and workflow before adding
software. Prove a small daily routine with approved exports, reconcile events to
that master record, and pursue integrations through the system owner. IFCA was
suggested by a survey respondent; it is not verified as Chin Hin's system. “No
new CRM, no consultant, no vendor” is the challenge constraint. The current
hosted prototype uses external infrastructure and AI services, so do not claim
it has zero external-service dependencies. Clarify deployment, internal
ownership and approved platform use before a production pilot.
