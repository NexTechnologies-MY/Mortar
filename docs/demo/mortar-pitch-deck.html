  • Suggests the next action and its urgency for a stalled case
  • Ranks reviewed playbooks by fit to the live blocker
  • Scores buyer responsiveness and hesitation from message gaps

People And Plain Math Decide
  • A person confirms every milestone — unconfirmed evidence never moves a case
  • Financing risk is deterministic rules, not a model
  • The forecast is stage rates and intervals, not generated prediction
  • Staff author and review every playbook
  • Executives cancel bookings and release units

17 / 20 · The Measure
The Measure

The One Number We Are Judged By.

30-Day Verified SPA Rate
Bookings that sign a verified SPA within 30 days ÷ eligible bookings in the cohort.
Definitive
A signed SPA is an executed contract of sale — 10% falls due on signing.
Readable In One Quarter
Enroll a cohort between day 15 and 44; observe by day 74; reconcile by 84.
Forces The Behaviour
Unblock early, or release the unit while the campaign still fits it.
If follow-up lifts 40 stalled bookings from 20% to 40%, that is +8 signed SPAs — a planning assumption to replace with pilot data. Never converted to cash.

The Path To Proof

A 12-Week Pilot.

WEEKS 1–2
Intake & Baseline
Existing booking exports, one project, one Sales Admin. Reconcile closed cases; capture the first playbooks.
WEEKS 3–4
Assisted Queue
The daily chase list with one administrator. Jev extraction where it removes a burden.
WEEKS 5–8
Live Cohort
Bookings from day 15–44 get the assisted queue; a comparable group keeps ordinary follow-up.
WEEKS 9–12
Audit & Decide
Verified SPAs with Legal. Compare cohorts; count signed SPAs and inventory days released.
Inside the brief's rules: no new CRM, no consultant, no vendor — one project, existing channels, twelve weeks.
